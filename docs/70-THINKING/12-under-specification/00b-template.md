# Per-paper review sheet — template

*The format for each `NN-<slug>.md` review sheet in a topic folder. Proposed, not
required: some papers carry more material than others; explore each as far as it
deserves. Primary sources only — read the actual paper, not summaries or secondary
coverage. Write in full sentences, close to what the paper reports, away from
editorialising. This is the collect phase — no conclusions across papers; that
comes later, finding by finding, in `00d-findings.md`.*

## Density

A one-line flag at the very top of every sheet, before anything else: **HIGH /
MEDIUM / LOW**, plus a sentence. It records two things — how much of the paper is
load-bearing signal rather than restatement, boilerplate, or padding; and how
much of that signal actually bears on the Subject. A dense catalogue with little
on the Subject is LOW here; a short paper that is all signal on the Subject is
HIGH.

## Body

- **Name** — and **keywords** (the reviewer's own, not the paper's), **year**,
  **venue**.
- **Approach** — what they did.
- **Model(s) targeted** and **benchmarks used**.
- **Author incentive** — affiliation, funding, product or position stake.
- **Measurement methodology** — brushed, not exhaustive: the dependent variable,
  what is controlled, N, how significance is treated.
- **Key findings** — including **the authors' own reading** of them.
- **Open questions the paper itself raises** — its stated limitations and
  future-work, in its own framing.
- **Appreciation** — the reviewer's assessment, keeping **MECHANISM separate from
  MAGNITUDE**: which findings transfer as an argument, which are numbers valid
  only inside the paper's own setup.
- **How it could serve a harness-design effort / limitations / major concerns** —
  stem the discussion, do not resolve it.
- **Five citations** worth chasing for the next round.
