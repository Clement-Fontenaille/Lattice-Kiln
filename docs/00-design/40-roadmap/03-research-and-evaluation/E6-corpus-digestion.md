# E6 — Does the system hold and use knowledge it did not derive itself?

**Status:** not run.
**Gated on:** [M8](../01-MILESTONES/08-persistent-work-and-knowledge.md) having
something to run. Not gated on E0—E5 — this corpus and the M6 suite are different
instruments for different questions.

## The question

Feed the `70-THINKING/` literature-review corpus — the F-numbered findings across
topics 07 and 08, the I-numbered ideas register — through the knowledge model once a
processor exists to do the ingesting, and ask whether the system holds and correctly
uses knowledge it did not derive itself in the same session.

**This is the first test the "curated memory beats conversational accumulation"
hypothesis has had.**

## Design

Small tests first — a processor reuses one stored finding and measurably beats one
starting cold — then the corpus sweep. Sequencing matters: a corpus sweep that fails
tells you nothing about which of ingestion, retention or retrieval failed.

## What it is graded against

M9's metrics once those exist — validity, reliability, pertinence, scope — **not task
success alone**, since the corpus was not produced to serve any one task. Grading it
on task success would measure whether the corpus happens to help the M6 suite, which
is a different and less interesting question.

## What it also produces

The first corpus large enough to ask whether incremental deletion stays cheap
(`10-technical/12-knowledge-model.md`).
